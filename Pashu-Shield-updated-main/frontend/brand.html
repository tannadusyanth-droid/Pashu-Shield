Pashu Mitra brand guidelines

Designed to conform to DBIM 3.0 (5.2.4 Lockup 3B, 5.3, 5.5) and GIGW 3.0; CQW/STQC certification pending audit. This is an independent initiative and not an official Government of India website. Logos are original artwork; no State Emblem or government logo is used.

Lockups (single colour only)

Rules

RuleValue
HeaderBlack lockup on white. Footer: white lockup on key colour #0F5757. No other colour variants.
Clear space2 horn-stem widths (12 units, about 0.21 × mark height) on every side, enforced by logo.js.
Minimum sizeHorizontal lockup 57 px high; mark only 30 px. Below the minimum the mark-only variant is used automatically.
NeverStretch, condense, rotate, outline, hollow, add shadow, bevel, glow, gradient or texture; redraw; place on busy photography. Each logo file stays under 100 KB.
Co-brandingAt most 2 co-brand logos in the header, supplied and authorised by their owners. Pashu Mitra stays left (horizontal) or on top (vertical).
Use in codePMLogo.html({ variant, tone, lang, height, context }). No style, class, filter or transform input exists.
File namespashumitra_<standard|alternate|mark>_<eng|hin|xxx>_<black|white>.svg
